# Pricing and subscription transparency of AI companion apps (2026)

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.7910%2FDVN%2FN4NPYF-blue.svg)](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/N4NPYF)
[![Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23866139.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23866139)
[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

What does an AI companion app tell you about its prices, credits, automatic renewal, refunds and age checks before you pay? This dataset compares 20 consumer AI companion apps, general audience and adult-oriented, observed on one day (1 November 2026) from Belgium: public pages, one free account per app where e-mail registration was possible, and the upgrade flow up to the screen that collects a payment method. No payment was made.

## Key figures

- **Price before sign-up:** 8 of 20 apps show no plan or price until an account exists.
- **Pricing model:** 17 of 20 combine a subscription with credits, tokens, gems or coins sold on top; 3 sell a plain subscription.
- **Cheapest paid plan:** from 3.33 USD to 14.92 EUR per month (monthly equivalent of the cheapest plan), median 5.83. Median annual discount 52 percent.
- **Automatic renewal:** stated on the payment screen for 9 of the 14 checkouts reached; 4 leave it to the terms; 1 states that nothing renews; 6 checkouts could not be reached under the rules of the study.
- **Refunds:** 17 of 20 apps state in their terms that payments are final, non-refundable or not refunded for unused time; 5 mention the EU 14-day withdrawal right.
- **Age gate:** a checkbox, button or interest selector for 12 apps, a date of birth or age bracket for 4, a sentence only for 4, no identity check anywhere.

All figures are recomputed from the CSV by `scripts/findings.py`; `scripts/check_dataset.py` validates the file.

## Method in short

One observer, one day (2026-11-01, UTC), one country (Belgium, no VPN), one browser. For each app: public pages, then a free account with a neutral persona where e-mail registration was possible, then the upgrade flow to the screen that collects a payment method, never further. Terms of service, refund pages and FAQ were read for cancellation and refund rules. Screens that could not be reached are coded `not observable` with the reason. Private evidence (screenshots, text dumps) and test account credentials are not published.

Prices are those displayed on the observation day in the currency served to a visitor from Belgium. They are not a price list and may have changed since. The dataset contains no personal data and no content generated by the apps.
